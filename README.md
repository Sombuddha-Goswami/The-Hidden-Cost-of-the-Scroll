The Hidden Cost of the Scroll

How Social Media Rewires Productivity — A Data-Driven Analysis

Overview

This study analyzes 1000 individuals across 7 age groups, 9 social media platforms, and 64 behavioral and psychological variables to quantify the hidden relationship between social media usage, dopamine dysregulation, and workplace productivity. 

Dataset

1,000 participants | 64 variables | 7 categories

Key Findings

53% productivity drop at 5+ hours/day
TikTok shows 4.9x higher dopamine dysregulation than LinkedIn
Daily doomscrollers: 9 min attention span (84% reduction)
Late-night scrolling costs 2.9 hours of sleep
60% of detox failures caused by boredom
Addiction risk increases with age (4.8x higher for 60+ vs teens)
Same work hours, dramatically less output
